Scope: whole app (Operate) with scene-led mastheads on the home and shared-bill pages. Friends and classmates splitting bills on phones, often at night, often inside LINE.
Direction is pinned by the user: the ICE17 x AI05 "Byenior" campaign's language, original artwork, the app's green. Intensity: scene plus calm tools. Dark and light both ship. Code-led (no image generation here).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bill split should feel like the group's own party poster until the moment money is on screen, then be perfectly legible. Refuses the category default of a soft fintech card stack, and refuses the outgoing frosted glass.

OWN-WORLD: Surreal Y2K collage: big sky, perspective emerald checkerboard, tall grass, oversized flowers, glossy green chess pieces, a lilac retro computer showing a pixel divide sign. Ultra-wide black caps (Anybody Expanded; Kanit for Thai). Solid, flat working panels with small corners, checker rules, no blur anywhere. Day: cobalt sky, white-and-emerald board. Night: near-black sky, black-and-emerald board.

STORY: The visitor sees the poster and the app's name, scans a receipt, moves a pawn along a rank of five squares through the steps, and shares a link whose preview card is the same poster. A friend opening it sees the bill title and total on the scene, then what they owe on a calm panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: full-bleed scene about 46% of screen height, BILL SPLITTER in huge wide caps over the sky, server-rendered so it paints before JavaScript. Below it the five-square rank, then the New split panel with Scan as the primary action; the fixed dock with the green camera square sits at the bottom. Once a draft has items or is past step one the scene collapses to a slim strip.

FORM: Pinned reference world (user-chosen), translated to green. Seed key dcb4f1a9, pinned brief beats the roll; signature interaction: the pawn advances square by square and promotes to a queen on Share.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
